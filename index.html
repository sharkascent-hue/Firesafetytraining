Fire Safety Legislation & Compliance

Fire safety training is a legal requirement

Under Irish law, every business must give its staff adequate fire safety training.

Fire Services Acts 1981 & 2003 Safety, Health & Welfare at Work Act 2005

As a property owner or employer, you have a "Duty of Care" to:

  1. Take reasonable measures to prevent fires breaking out.
  2. Provide fire safety equipment and clear emergency procedures.
  3. Make sure staff are trained in evacuation and the use of fire equipment.

Special requirements for care homes

For designated centres and nursing homes, our training meets the strict requirements of Regulation 28 of the Health Act 2007. We cover the specific emergency procedures, evacuation techniques and resident safety you need to stay fully compliant with HIQA standards.

HIQA · Health Act 2007, Regulation 28

Book an Assessment

Our fire safety audit covers every critical element of your premises to ensure full legal compliance. The inspection includes a detailed review of:

Life safety systems

Full testing and inspection of the fire alarm control panel, smoke and heat detectors, and emergency lighting.

Essential fire equipment

Visual and physical checks of all firefighting equipment and extinguishers.

Structural protection

Assessment of fire doors, escape routes and primary means of escape.

Clearance & visibility

Verification of fire safety signage and designated assembly points.

Hazard control

Review of heating systems, electrical installations and potential ignition sources.

Readiness & compliance

Evaluation of fire drills, staff training needs and mandatory fire safety records.

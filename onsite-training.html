Fire Safety Legislation & Compliance

Fire safety training is a legal requirement

Under the Fire Services Acts (1981 & 2003) and the Safety, Health and Welfare at Work Act 2005, every business must give its staff adequate fire safety training.

Fire Services Acts 1981 & 2003Safety, Health & Welfare at Work Act 2005

As a property owner or employer, you have a "Duty of Care" to:

  1. Take reasonable measures to prevent fires breaking out.
  2. Provide fire safety equipment and clear emergency procedures.
  3. Make sure staff are trained in evacuation and the use of fire equipment.

Special requirements for care homes

For designated centres and nursing homes, our training meets the strict requirements of Regulation 28 of the Health Act 2007. We cover the specific emergency procedures, evacuation techniques and resident safety you need to stay fully compliant with HIQA standards.

HIQA · Health Act 2007, Regulation 28

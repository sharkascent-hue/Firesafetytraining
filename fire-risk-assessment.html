What We Inspect

A comprehensive fire safety audit

Our audit covers every critical element of your premises to ensure full legal compliance. The inspection includes a detailed review of:

Life safety systems

Full testing and inspection of the fire alarm control panel, smoke and heat detectors, and emergency lighting.

Essential fire equipment

Visual and physical checks of all firefighting equipment and extinguishers.

Structural protection

Assessment of fire doors, escape routes and primary means of escape.

Clearance & visibility

Verification of fire safety signage and designated assembly points.

Hazard control

Review of heating systems, electrical installations and potential ignition sources.

Readiness & compliance

Evaluation of fire drills, staff training needs and mandatory fire safety records.

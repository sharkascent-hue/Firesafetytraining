Our Story

Expertise from the front line

With over 60 years' combined experience as operational firefighters between our two directors, we use that expertise to give every customer the best possible service and the highest standard of fire safety training.

Our aim is to give you the skills and knowledge to practise fire safety at home and at work. Our courses help you understand the dangers of fire, and give you the confidence and ability to follow the right procedures to evacuate and help others.

With offices in Dublin, Sligo, Limerick and Galway, we travel nationwide delivering courses in Fire Awareness, Fire Warden/Marshal, Evacuation and Fire Extinguishers.

Meet the Team

Fire safety experts, Ireland

Founder

Pat Kirby

Pat has a lifetime of fire safety experience, having served 32 years with the Airport Police & Fire Service at Shannon International Airport, the last 10 as Chief Fire & Police Officer.

In 2002 he moved Kirby Fire Safety into its current premises at Smithstown, Shannon, Co. Clare. Pat holds a Senior Fire Officer Certificate from the UK Civil Aviation Authority and a Breathing Apparatus Instructor's Certificate from Lancashire County Fire Service. In November 2002 he was awarded a Diploma in Training & Education by the National University of Ireland, Galway.

Head Instructor

Enda Kirby

Enda has 26 years' experience as a firefighter and has delivered fire safety courses for 25 years. He began his career with Clare County Fire Service in Ennis in 1993, then joined Galway City Fire Service as a full-time firefighter in 2001.

In the fire service, Enda completed courses in Breathing Apparatus, Compartment Fire Behaviour, Wildland Firefighting, Swift Water Rescue, Heights Rescue, Road Traffic Collision Extrication, Aviation Firefighting and Manual Handling, and he is a First Responder. He holds a Certificate in Occupational Health & Safety from NISO and a QQI Level 6 Component Award in Train the Trainer.
